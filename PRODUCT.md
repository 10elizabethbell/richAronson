# Product: Rich Aronson — small moves + cleanouts

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site; the visitor decides and texts or calls)

## Users
- People in New Jersey (732 area code, towns unpublished) moving out of an apartment, condo or a few rooms, who need a van and a strong pair of hands, not a full moving company. (inferred from "specialize in Small Moves")
- Homeowners, landlords and families clearing out a house, garage or basement who already have, or can get, a dumpster and need the labor to fill it. (from "You get a dumpster and we provide the labor to fill it.")
- Anyone with one or a few pieces of usable furniture they want gone. (from "I can make it 'disappear'")
- They find the link on a phone, mostly from a Facebook group post, inside Facebook's in-app browser.

## Purpose
Give Rich's business its first web footprint: a page a customer can open from a Facebook post and text or call Rich from in one tap. Secondly, show Rich his own business done better than he imagined.

## Positioning
One guy, one large Sprinter van, small moves done personally. Not a moving company with a fleet; the person who answers the text is the person who carries the couch.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: call or text 732-682-7345 (brief: "call or text Rich"). No email, no booking link, no form backend.
- Services (verbatim): small moves with a large Sprinter van; clean outs (customer gets the dumpster, Rich provides the labor to fill it); making one or a few pieces of usable furniture "disappear".
- Unknown: business name, service towns, hours, prices, years in business, reviews, photos, logo, colors.
- No business name is published. The page uses the owner's real name ("Rich Aronson") as the name until Rich confirms a business name. Do not invent one.
- Do not claim licensing or insurance.

## Brand commitments
- Tone: casual, direct, a little playful ("MOVING Soon ? ?", "I can make it 'disappear'", 💪😎💪). (from the ad)
- Speak as Rich, first person. The ad mixes "We have" and "I can"; use "I" for the personal promise and "we" only where the ad does.
- Never a black page ground.
